## Full post

**🇺🇸 PATRIOTS — TALK TIME IS HOME AT LAST**

I have been waiting a long time to write this one.

Talk Time is now **inside our own community**. Not on Discord. Not on Telegram.
Not on Signal. Not on somebody else's platform that can shut us down on a
Tuesday afternoon because they don't care for what we have to say.

**It's ours. It's here. And nobody can take it from us.**

For years we have been pushed from one outside app to another. Every move, good
people got locked out and never made it back. Some of you have been trying to
get into Talk Time since 2023. Some of you gave up. I do not blame you one bit.

**That ends today.**

---

**HOW TO GET IN — three steps, that's all**

1. Click the button below to open **Talk Time 2.0**
2. Sign in with your **Google account** and pick **your state**
3. That's it. You're in.

No invite links to hunt for. No codes that expire before you can click them. No
making your name match on some other app. Just click, sign in, and you're home.

---

**WHAT YOU CAN DO IN THERE**

🇺🇸 Talk with patriots **nationwide**
🗺️ Talk with the folks in **your own state**
🎙️ Join the **live talks**

Your state group is right there with you now. One place. One sign-in.

---

**ONE HONEST WORD**

This is brand new. We built it for us, and things that are brand new sometimes
need a nudge. If something does not work the way you expect, **do not sit there
frustrated and do not give up on us** — that is the one thing I would hate.

Tell us in the chat and we will get it sorted. Every single report makes this
thing better for the patriot coming in behind you.

---

We have been through a lot together. Migrations, shutdowns, lockouts, all of it.
And you stuck with us anyway.

**Welcome home. Come on in — I'll see you in there.**

**— Chas** 🇺🇸
